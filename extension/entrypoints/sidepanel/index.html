JobLens

When you click "Analyze this posting", JobLens sends that posting's text to the backend URL you configure. Your backend forwards it to Jev (TypeSafe AI) for classification, and - only for postings that pass your configured gate - to a third-party LLM provider (OpenRouter) for talking points. Nothing is sent automatically, and only the one posting you're viewing is ever sent.

Set your backend URL in JobLens options before analyzing a posting.
